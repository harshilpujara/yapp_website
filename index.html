Privacy

There's no yapp server.
Seriously.

Your audio goes straight from your PC to the provider you picked, with your own key. It never comes to us, because there's nowhere for it to go.

  • Each recording is deleted from your PC as soon as it's sent
  • No history of what you said. Stats count words, never store them
  • No tracking, no analytics, no crash reporting
  • Your API key lives in Windows Credential Manager

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

What does it cost me?

yapp is free. You use your own API key and pay your provider for what you use. Groq has a free tier that is enough for many people. No subscription, no hidden fees.

Where does my audio go?

Directly from your PC to the provider you chose, using your own key. It never goes to us, because there is no yapp server. Each recording is deleted from your PC as soon as it has been sent, and yapp keeps no history of what you said. Your provider has its own privacy policy for what they do with what you send.

Which providers work?

Any service with an OpenAI-compatible API, including Groq, OpenAI and OpenRouter. Groq is the easiest start: create a key, paste it in, done.

Which languages work?

Many, including English, Hindi, Gujarati, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. The exact list depends on your provider's speech model. yapp never translates: you get back the language you spoke.

Does it work offline?

No. Transcription and cleanup are done by your provider over the internet, so you need a connection.

Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now. yapp is open source, so if you'd like to help bring it elsewhere, the repo is the place to start.

How do I update it?

Download the newer installer from Releases and run it over the old one. Your settings and key are kept.
