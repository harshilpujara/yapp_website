Privacy

Your voice stays yours.

Audio goes straight from your PC to the speech provider you chose, using your own key. There's no yapp server in the middle, so there's nothing of yours for us to keep.

Your PC Your provider
Deleted after sending
Each recording is removed from your PC as soon as it's been sent.
No history
yapp keeps no record of what you said. The dashboard counts words, it doesn't store them.
No tracking
No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting.
Key stays local
Your API key is stored in Windows Credential Manager, never in a settings file.

Pricing

Free. Actually free.

No subscription, no account, no trial. You use your own API key and pay your provider for what you use, which is usually very little. Groq's free tier is enough for a lot of people.

FAQ

Good questions.

Something else? Open an issue →

What does it cost?

yapp itself is free. You use your own API key and pay your provider directly for what you use. Groq has a free tier that's enough for many people. There's no subscription and no hidden fees.

Where does my audio go?

Directly from your PC to the provider you chose. It never goes to us, because there is no yapp server. Each recording is deleted from your PC as soon as it's sent, and yapp keeps no history of what you said. Your provider has its own privacy policy for what they do with it.

Which providers work?

Any service with an OpenAI-compatible API, including Groq, OpenAI and OpenRouter. Groq is the easiest place to start.

Which languages work?

Many, including English, Hindi, Gujarati, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Chinese. The exact list depends on your provider's speech model.

Does it work offline?

No. Transcription and cleanup are done by your provider over the internet, so you need a connection.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet, it's Windows only for now. The project is open source if you'd like to help.

How do I update?

Download the newer installer from Releases and run it over the old one. Your settings and key are kept.
